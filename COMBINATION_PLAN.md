# Zv2 product direction

Zv2 combines settlement growth, map exploration and room-scale zombie survival in
one standalone game. Civilization mechanics provide the long arc: gather resources,
specialize survivors, research technology and expand a fortified stronghold. Survival
mechanics provide immediate decisions: reveal dangerous locations, clear infected
rooms, recover supplies and survive escalating nights.

The implementation and content are owned by Zv2. Inspiration from earlier strategy
games is limited to general mechanics and does not create a runtime or data dependency.
